ABOUT THE PROJECT

The FrontDesk Management System digitizes and streamlines reception operations across multiple office locations.

It enables secure visitor registration, real-time tracking, employee notifications, visitor pass generation, and automated reporting.

Improved front-desk operational efficiency

Centralized visibility across locations

THE PROBLEM

  • Manual and fragmented reception processes
  • Need for reliable visitor verification
  • Difficulty tracking visitors throughout their visit
  • Need for faster visitor registration
  • Need to notify the employee being visited
  • Limited centralized visibility for management
  • Need to standardize operations across multiple locations

THE SOLUTION

  • Digitized and standardized FrontDesk operations across locations
  • Track visitors from entry to exit
  • Verify visitors using OTP authentication
  • Generate visitor passes after successful verification
  • Support receptionist-assisted and QR-code self check-in
  • Automate employee notifications
  • Provide centralized visitor data and analytical reports

USERS & STAKEHOLDERS

👤 Receptionist

Manages visitor check-in / check-out, appointments, visitor register, and visitor pass generation.

📊 Report Viewer

Accesses reports, exports data and analyzes visitor information.

🧑‍💼 Management

Views dashboards, KPIs and operational reports.

🙋 Visitor

Provides information, completes verification and receives visitor pass.

🔔 Employee / Host

Receives notification when the visitor checks in.

👥 Business & Ops Teams

Define requirements and monitor the effectiveness of the system.
